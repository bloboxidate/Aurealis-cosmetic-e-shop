Our Story
Light doesn't have one color. Neither does your glow.
Aurealis began on a winter night beneath the aurora borealis — watching color move across the sky in a way no single pigment could hold. We wanted skincare and makeup that felt like that: alive, shifting, never flat.
Every formula is built around clean, skin-loving ingredients and a palette drawn directly from the northern lights — sage, azure, lavender, honey, and apricot glow, woven through everything we make.
